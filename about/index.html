About Megan

I make high-stakes software feel obvious, from research all the way into the code.

I have a thing for messy problems: the "wait, how does this even work?" kind of software. At Johnson & Johnson, that means leading the design for the platform that tracks personalized cell therapies from patient to lab and back for around 6,000 people and 10,000+ patients. I've turned its complex, intimidating workflows into something that feels simple and obvious. I own it end to end, from research through the design system, and I'm fluent enough in front-end code to help build what I design instead of only handing it off.
