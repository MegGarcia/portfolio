At Work Off The Clock

I have a thing for messy problems: the “wait, how does this even work?” kind of software. At Johnson & Johnson, I lead design for the platform that tracks personalized cell therapies from patient to lab and back, serving around 6,000 users and 10,000+ patients. I’ve made its intimidating workflows feel simple and obvious, and I own it end to end, from research to design system. I’m also fluent enough in front-end code to help build what I design, not just hand it off.
