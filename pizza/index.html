Pizza Shop Process Deck

What if ordering pizza felt like a nostalgic video game? A custom kiosk that lets customers build their perfect pizza, inspired by early 2000s cooking mini-games.

The Problem

At Pi Craft, you build your own pizza at a long counter of dough, sauce, cheese and toppings. For its core customers, families and college students, that moment came with three problems.

01

Too many options, no labels

Nothing on the counter told you what you were looking at.

02

Pressure from the line

Deciding with people waiting behind you is stressful.

03

A counter too tall for kids

Little ones couldn't see the options they were choosing.

02

Two Key Decisions

A kiosk built for everyone

I designed a custom kiosk with a square screen shaped like a pizza box, plus a card reader, receipt printer and loyalty scanner. It sits at an ADA-compliant height on a ledge for personal items, and the screen tilts down so people of all heights can reach it.

A 2000s cooking-game look

To win over families and college students, I leaned into the playful energy of early 2000s cooking games. I hand-drew every dough, sauce, cheese and topping and paired them with Londrina Solid type to match Pi Craft's funky logo.

03

The Process

As a solo project, I took the kiosk from business analysis to high-fidelity screens:

  • Workflow diagram: mapped how a kiosk fits Pi Craft's current order process
  • Physical mockup: tested the tilt, height, and button and text sizes
  • Style guide: burnt orange and gray palette, playful type, and 4- and 5-column grids

The Outcome

Pi Craft now has an accessible second way to order and customize pizza. The playful look appeals to families and college students, and the live pizza preview takes the guesswork and pressure out of building one.
