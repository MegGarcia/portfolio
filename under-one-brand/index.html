The Problem

In 2023, Janssen became Johnson & Johnson, but our platform still carried the Janssen logo. The deeper issue: it answered to three identities at once, and the ~10% of users who also use CQUENCE® moved between two products that felt like strangers.

01

A legal name nobody used

"COI/COC" described the regulated process, not the product.

02

A borrowed name everyone used

Users called it "CQUENCE®" after its shared login, a brand we didn't own.

03

A look that was no one's brand

Default Salesforce blue, mistaken internally for our own.

02

Two Key Decisions

Earning the name

Claiming the trusted CQUENCE® name was legally fuzzy as it had already been trademarked by another J&J digital product, and getting it wrong was a compliance risk. Over two years of sessions with leadership and legal, I cleared the rebrand rights, secured the name and won funding. The result was "CQUENCE® Chain of Identity & Custody."

Translating J&J to Salesforce

J&J's two design systems weren't built for a data-heavy Salesforce app, and their tokens contradicted each other. I went native-first: real Salesforce components wherever they matched, semi-custom ones only for the gaps, and one documented set of tokens as the source of truth.

03

The Build

As the only designer, I hand-coded most of the system in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, working alongside four in-house and two agency engineers. I personally owned and delivered:

  • The token system: reconciled across both J&J systems
  • The component library: navbar, table, date picker, form fields, checklist navigation and more
  • The pages: landing, completed order summary, shipment tracking, upcoming orders and legal pages
  • Accessibility gates: no page moved on without a green Lighthouse AA score
04

What Leadership Said

"The redesign didn't just refresh the platform. It showed me what it should have looked like all along."

A VP seeing it for the first time. Leadership agreed it finally "feels like J&J."

05

The Outcome

The platform is fully off default Salesforce and onto a true J&J identity, unified with the CQUENCE® ecosystem. It shipped in September 2026, and a user survey in November 2026 will measure the shift.
